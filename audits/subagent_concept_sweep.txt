## THE 3 WORKS CLOSEST TO SCD's FIVE-FACTOR CHAIN
1. **DAR (2603.20640)** — the only multi-agent LLM reasoning method with an explicit immutability mechanism (index-based retention keeps originals unchanged); lacks speculative branch, delta, and validator.
2. **TomasuLLM (2609.38201)** — the only paper instantiating the full speculative chain (copy-on-write speculative branch → validation against committed state → commit); but for a single agent's tool calls, not peer reasoning.
3. **DeepRewind (2609.36344)** — the only paper with an explicit binary COMMIT-blocking controller + dependency-aware ROLLBACK over a persistent epistemic state; but self-induced, single-agent.

**Bottom line**: no 2024–2026 paper combines all five factors; SCD's specific combination — peer info routed into a per-agent speculative branch, Δ_i as a first-class transition, and an explicit COMMIT/ROLLBACK validator inside multi-agent LLM reasoning — appears unclaimed. The nearest collisions are on individual factors: message immutability in MAD (DAR), speculative-branch-plus-validator semantics (TomasuLLM, Cordon, CoAgent), and commit/rollback controllers (DeepRewind, RIR). Peer-influence gating exists only in soft forms (R²-MAD confidence weights, self-social weighting 2601.05606), never as a binary validator over a represented transition.
